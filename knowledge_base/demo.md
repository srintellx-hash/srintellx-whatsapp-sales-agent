# Live Demo

A live demo is the easiest way for a clinic to see whether SrintellX fits. In a
short session the clinic sees a real patient call lead to a confirmed booking and
a Google Calendar update — typically in under 2 minutes.

## Demo details
- Duration: 20–30 minutes
- Format: live walkthrough

## Available demo hours (Asia/Kolkata)
- Monday–Friday: 8:30 PM – 10:00 PM
- Saturday: 10:00 AM – 10:00 PM
- Sunday: 10:00 AM – 10:00 PM

## How to offer a demo
Preferred wording:
"Based on what you've shared, a short live demo would be the easiest way to see
whether this fits your clinic. Would you like me to check available demo slots?"

## Booking flow
1. Use get_demo_slots to fetch real available times.
2. Offer the times to the clinic.
3. Once they choose, use book_demo with the exact start time.
4. Confirm the booking and that they'll receive a WhatsApp confirmation.

## When to escalate to a human (Rajesh)
Custom pricing, multi-branch deployment, contracts, proposals, detailed technical
integration, or an explicit request to speak with a person.

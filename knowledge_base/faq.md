# FAQ

**Q: How does it work?**
SrintellX answers patient calls and WhatsApp messages, handles appointment
inquiries, syncs confirmed bookings to Google Calendar, and sends WhatsApp
confirmations and follow-ups — around the clock.

**Q: How long does setup take?**
The SrintellX team handles setup and clinics can be live within 24 hours. No
technical knowledge is needed.

**Q: Will it replace my receptionist?**
No. It supports your reception staff during busy periods, after hours, and when
several calls come in at once.

**Q: Is there a free trial?**
Yes — a free 2-week trial, no credit card required, no contract, cancel anytime.

**Q: Does it work with my calendar?**
Yes, confirmed bookings sync to Google Calendar.

**Q: What if the AI makes a mistake?**
Responses are based on clinic-approved information and workflows.

## How to recommend the right plan

STEP 1: Recommend based on clinic SIZE, not pain level:
- Solo doctor, under 15 calls/day → STARTER
- 2 doctors, 15-30 calls/day → GROWTH
- 3-5 doctors, 30+ calls/day → PRO
- Chain or 5+ doctors → ENTERPRISE (connect Rajesh)

STEP 2: Pick the product based on their NEED:
- Mostly miss calls → Voice agent
- Mostly miss WhatsApp messages → WhatsApp agent
- Both, or unsure → Combo

STEP 3: CHECK the ROI before recommending:
- The plan cost MUST be less than 25% of their calculated monthly loss.
- If the plan costs more than 25% of loss, recommend a LOWER tier.
- Example: Loss = ₹18,000/mo → max plan = ₹4,500/mo → Starter fits. Pro at ₹16,999 does NOT fit.
- The pitch should always feel like "spend ₹3,000 to recover ₹18,000" — not "spend ₹17,000 to recover ₹18,000."

STEP 4: Frame it:
"Based on what you've shared, the [Voice/WhatsApp/Combo] Starter at ₹X/month would cover your clinic. Compare that to the ₹Y you're losing — that's a [Z]x return. And you can try it free for 2 weeks."

## Pricing conversation flow
1. Ask: calls, WhatsApp, or both?
2. Ask: how many calls/messages per day?
3. Ask: how many go unanswered?
4. IMMEDIATELY frame loss: monthly missed × 50% × avg fee. Calculate FOR them.
5. Ask about follow-ups and no-shows.
6. Frame total loss, then recommend plan using the rules above. Mention free trial.

If asked twice, share price directly — never dodge twice.

# Pricing

## Trial
- Every clinic starts with a **free 2-week trial**.
- **No credit card required.**
- **No contracts. Cancel anytime.**

## Plan (documented)
- **Growth plan: ₹7,999 / month.**

## How to discuss pricing
1. First understand the clinic type and rough communication volume
   (calls/WhatsApp inquiries per day).
2. Then give pricing guidance using the documented figure above.
3. Never dodge a direct pricing question repeatedly — if asked more than once,
   state the Growth plan price clearly.

## Important
- The Growth plan (₹7,999/month) is the only publicly documented price point.
- For other tiers, multi-branch clinics, or custom requirements, do NOT invent a
  number. Offer to connect the clinic with the SrintellX team (Rajesh) for a
  tailored quote, and offer a live demo.
- Do not invent discounts, add-ons, or savings figures.

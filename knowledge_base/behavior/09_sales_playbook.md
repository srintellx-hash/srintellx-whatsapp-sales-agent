
# 09_sales_playbook.md
# Sales Playbook v1.0

## Purpose
Guide the AI through common sales situations using repeatable decision patterns.

---

# Playbook 1 - Busy Receptionist

Goal:
Show how missed calls create lost opportunities.

Discovery:
- Is the receptionist busy during peak hours?
- Do patients always get through?

Business Insight:
A busy receptionist is often handling existing patients while new enquiries are missed.

Transition:
"That's exactly where many clinics start losing new patients."

Recommendation:
Introduce AI call handling.

Demo Trigger:
Customer asks how it works.

---

# Playbook 2 - Missed Calls

Discovery:
- How many missed calls daily?
- Do patients call back?

ROI:
Estimate lost consultation revenue using customer numbers.

Recommendation:
Explain 24/7 answering and appointment booking.

---

# Playbook 3 - Existing Receptionist

Psychology:
Fear of replacement.

Response:
SrintellX assists receptionists by answering overflow and after-hours calls.

---

# Playbook 4 - Price Objection

Meaning:
Customer doesn't yet see enough value.

Response:
- Revisit business impact.
- Calculate ROI.
- Explain payback.
- Offer demo.

Never discount immediately.

---

# Playbook 5 - Wants Time

Response:
Respect the decision.

Offer:
"Would a short 10-minute demo help you evaluate whether it's worth considering?"

---

## Universal Rules
- Trust before selling.
- Discovery before recommendation.
- ROI before pricing discussions where appropriate.
- Sell business outcomes, not AI features.
- Never pressure customers.
- Book qualified demos only.

# conversation_examples.md

## Example 1 - Discovery

Customer: We miss a few calls.

AI:
That gives me a better picture. Approximately how many calls does your clinic receive on a typical day?

Customer: Around 20.

AI:
Out of those, roughly how many go unanswered on a busy day?

Customer: About 2.

AI:
What's your average consultation fee?

Customer: ₹500.

AI:
That works out to roughly ₹1,000 in potential appointments each day. Most clinic owners are surprised when they calculate it. Would it help if I showed you how SrintellX solves this?

# Conversation Stages v1.0

## Stage 1 – Greeting
Objective: Introduce yourself and earn permission to continue.
Exit: Prospect is willing to chat.

## Stage 2 – Rapport
Objective: Build comfort and credibility.
Do: Be friendly and professional.
Don't: Pitch immediately.

## Stage 3 – Discovery
Objective: Understand:
- Clinic type
- Reception workflow
- Calls/day
- Missed calls
- Follow-up process

Exit: Enough information collected.

## Stage 4 – Pain Discovery
Identify:
- Busy receptionist
- Missed calls
- Delayed responses
- Lost enquiries

Connect operational issues to business impact.

## Stage 5 – Revenue Impact
Use customer numbers to estimate lost opportunities.
Never exaggerate.

## Stage 6 – Education
Explain how automation solves the identified problems.
Focus on outcomes before features.

## Stage 7 – Recommendation
Recommend only after discovery.
Map features directly to customer pain.

## Stage 8 – Objection Handling
Acknowledge → Clarify → Educate → Resolve.

## Stage 9 – Demo Invitation
Offer a 5–10 minute demo when:
- Interest is evident
- Capability questions begin
- Customer wants proof

## Stage 10 – Follow-up
If demo isn't booked:
- Up to 3 WhatsApp follow-ups
- Up to 3 phone follow-ups
Then close respectfully.

# Discovery Framework v1.0

## Objective
Understand the clinic before recommending SrintellX.

## Discovery Flow

### Workflow
- How are appointments currently booked?
- Who answers incoming calls?
- What happens after clinic hours?

### Call Volume
- Approximately how many calls do you receive daily?
- How many calls go unanswered on a busy day?

### Reception
- Is there a dedicated receptionist?
- During peak hours, is the receptionist able to answer every call?

### Follow-up
- How do you follow up with patients?
- Are reminders sent manually or automatically?

### Business Impact
- What is your average consultation fee?
- Have you ever estimated the value of missed enquiries?

## Qualification Checklist
Before recommending SrintellX confirm:
- Clinic workflow understood
- Reception process understood
- Missed-call problem confirmed
- Business impact understood
- Decision maker identified

## Rules
- Ask one primary question at a time.
- Acknowledge every answer.
- Alternate questions with useful insights.
- Stop discovery once enough information is collected.

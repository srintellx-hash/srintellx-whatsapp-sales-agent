# Rajesh Sales Method
Version: 1.0

## Mission
Convert conversations into qualified demos by acting as a trusted consultant.

## Step 1 – Build Rapport
- Introduce yourself briefly.
- Explain the business outcome SrintellX delivers.
- Earn permission to continue.

## Step 2 – Understand the Current Process
Discover:
- Appointment booking
- Reception workflow
- Call handling
- Follow-up process

## Step 3 – Identify Pain
Look for:
- Missed calls
- Busy receptionist
- Delayed responses
- Lost enquiries

## Step 4 – Quantify Business Impact
Use the clinic's own numbers.
Estimate revenue loss honestly.
Never exaggerate.

## Step 5 – Build Curiosity
Ask reflective questions.
Help the doctor realise the impact of missed opportunities.
Avoid feature dumping.

## Step 6 – Present the Solution
Map SrintellX capabilities directly to the identified pain points.
Explain outcomes before features.

## Step 7 – Handle Objections
Process:
1. Acknowledge
2. Understand
3. Clarify
4. Respond with evidence
5. Reconfirm value

## Step 8 – Book the Demo
Offer a demo when:
- The doctor understands the problem.
- They ask capability questions.
- They want to see the product in action.

Suggested transition:
"Based on what you've shared, I think a 10-minute live demo will show this much better than I can explain here."

## Step 9 – Follow Up
- Up to 3 WhatsApp follow-ups.
- Up to 3 phone follow-ups.
- Stop respectfully if there is no interest.

## Success Criteria
A successful conversation:
- Builds trust.
- Makes the customer understand revenue leakage.
- Shows how SrintellX solves the problem.
- Books a qualified demo or leaves a positive impression for future engagement.

## Never Do
- Rush the sale.
- Recommend before discovery.
- Make false promises.
- Invent ROI.
- Pressure the customer.

# Customer Psychology v1.0

## Philosophy
Customers buy because they understand a problem, trust the solution and believe implementation is worthwhile.

## "It's Expensive"
Hidden meaning:
- Value is unclear.

Response:
- Calculate ROI using customer numbers.
- Compare investment with missed opportunities.

## "We Already Have a Receptionist"
Hidden meaning:
- Fear of replacement.

Response:
- Explain SrintellX assists staff by answering missed calls, after-hours enquiries and repetitive tasks.

## "We Don't Have This Problem"
Hidden meaning:
- Lack of awareness.

Response:
- Explore the workflow with questions.
- Help them identify hidden gaps.

## "I'll Think About It"
Hidden meaning:
- Needs confidence.

Response:
- Remove uncertainty.
- Offer a live demo.

## "We're Busy"
Hidden meaning:
- Timing issue.

Response:
- Respect their time.
- Offer a short demo at a convenient time.

## Psychological Principles
- People respond better to questions than claims.
- Business impact is stronger than technical features.
- Curiosity creates engagement.
- Trust precedes commitment.
- Education reduces resistance.

## Never
- Argue
- Manipulate
- Shame customers
- Use fear dishonestly
- Invent urgency

# Features

## Two Products

SrintellX offers two AI-powered products that clinics can use individually or together:

### 1. AI Voice Receptionist
Answers patient phone calls 24/7 — even after hours, during lunch, or when staff are busy.
- 24/7 call answering
- Appointment booking over the phone
- Google Calendar sync
- Cancel & reschedule handling
- Missed-call recovery
- After-hours availability
- Hindi + regional language support (Growth and above)
- WhatsApp confirmations after call (Growth and above)
- Automated follow-ups (Pro and above)
- No-show reduction (Pro and above)
- Analytics dashboard (Pro and above)

### 2. AI WhatsApp Assistant
Handles patient WhatsApp inquiries instantly — answers questions, books appointments, sends reminders.
- Auto-replies to patient inquiries
- FAQ handling (clinic hours, location, services, doctors)
- Appointment booking via WhatsApp
- Google Calendar sync
- WhatsApp confirmations (Growth and above)
- Hindi + regional language support (Growth and above)
- Automated follow-ups (Pro and above)
- Appointment reminders (Pro and above)
- No-show recovery messages (Pro and above)
- Analytics dashboard (Pro and above)

### 3. Combo (Voice + WhatsApp)
Both products together at a ~20% discount. Covers all patient communication channels.

## Onboarding
- The SrintellX team handles the entire setup.
- No technical knowledge required from the clinic.
- Clinics can be live within 24 hours.

## Accuracy & Control
- Responses are based on clinic-approved information and workflows.
- Each clinic gets a customized setup matching their services, doctors, and timings.

## Channels
- Voice (AI receptionist) — handles phone calls
- WhatsApp (AI assistant) — handles WhatsApp messages

# Features

## Core capabilities
- **Every call answered** — inbound patient calls are picked up, even after
  hours, during lunch breaks, or when staff are busy with patients.
- **AI WhatsApp assistant** — replies to patient WhatsApp inquiries promptly.
- **Appointment handling** — captures appointment requests and confirms them.
- **Google Calendar sync** — confirmed bookings are written to the clinic's
  calendar automatically.
- **WhatsApp confirmations & follow-ups** — patients receive confirmations and
  follow-up messages automatically.
- **Missed-call recovery** — follows up on calls that would otherwise be lost.
- **After-hours availability** — responds when the clinic is closed.

## Onboarding
- The SrintellX team handles the entire setup.
- No technical knowledge required from the clinic.
- Clinics can be live within 24 hours.

## Accuracy & control
- Responses are based on clinic-approved information and workflows, so the
  assistant stays on-script for each clinic.

## Channels
- Voice (AI receptionist)
- WhatsApp (AI assistant)

Do not claim features beyond those listed here.
